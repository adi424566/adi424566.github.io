Account and Data Deletion

Game: VORA: Battlegrounds

Developer: Dark Signal

Request Account Deletion

Players can request permanent deletion of their VORA: Battlegrounds account and associated personal data by contacting our support team.

How to Submit a Request

  1. Open VORA: Battlegrounds.
  2. Open your player profile.
  3. Copy your player UID.
  4. Click the Request Account Deletion button below.
  5. Include your player UID and username.
  6. Send the email to our support team.

We may request additional information to verify account ownership before processing your request. Never share your password or login credentials.

REQUEST ACCOUNT DELETION

Data Associated With Your Account

Account deletion requests cover personal data associated with your account, including:

  • Player account and profile information.
  • Player UID and username.
  • Game progress and statistics.
  • Ranks and match history.
  • Virtual currency and inventory.
  • Friends list and associated social data.
Important: Permanent account deletion may result in the loss of all game progress, virtual currency, purchased items and rewards. Deleted accounts cannot be restored.

Data Retention

Certain information may be retained where necessary for legal compliance, security, fraud prevention or dispute resolution. We will explain any applicable retention requirements when processing your request.

Processing Time

Our support team will review your request, verify account ownership and inform you of the expected deletion timeline.
