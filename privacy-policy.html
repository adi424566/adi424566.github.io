Privacy Policy

VORA: Battlegrounds

Last updated: November 27, 2026

1. Introduction

Dark Signal develops VORA: Battlegrounds, an online multiplayer game for Android. This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected, used, processed and protected when you use the game.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, information processed by the game and its service providers may include:

  • Player account identifiers and guest login identifiers.
  • Player usernames, profiles, avatars and banners.
  • Gameplay statistics, ranks and match results.
  • Virtual currency and inventory information.
  • Friends lists and multiplayer interactions.
  • Device information, IP addresses and network information.
  • Advertising identifiers and advertisement interaction data.
  • Information provided when contacting customer support.

3. How We Use Information

  • To create and manage player accounts.
  • To provide multiplayer gameplay and matchmaking.
  • To save player progress and inventory.
  • To provide advertisements and in-game rewards.
  • To prevent cheating, fraud and abuse.
  • To maintain security and improve game performance.
  • To respond to player support requests.

4. Third-Party Services

VORA: Battlegrounds uses third-party services to provide authentication, multiplayer networking, backend functionality and advertising. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

  • Microsoft PlayFab: Microsoft Privacy Statement
  • Photon Fusion and Photon Chat: Photon Engine
  • Google AdMob: Google Privacy Policy

5. Advertising

The game uses Google AdMob to provide advertisements, including optional rewarded advertisements. Advertising services may process device identifiers, IP addresses and advertisement interaction data.

Advertising personalization and consent options may depend on your location, device settings and applicable privacy requirements.

Learn more about Google's advertising practices: Google Advertising Privacy

6. Multiplayer and Social Features

Your player username, avatar, rank and other public profile information may be visible to other players through multiplayer matches, friends lists and social features.

7. Data Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers as necessary to operate the game, provide advertisements, maintain security and comply with applicable legal obligations.

8. Data Retention

Player information is retained as necessary to provide game services, maintain account progress, resolve disputes, prevent abuse and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Players may request deletion of their game account and associated personal information by contacting our support email.
